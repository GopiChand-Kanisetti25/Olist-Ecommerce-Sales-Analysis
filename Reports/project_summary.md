# Olist E-Commerce Sales Analysis – Project Report

## 1. Project Overview
This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product categories, payment methods, and delivery performance.

## 2. Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Jupyter Notebook
- MySQL
- Power BI

## 3. Key Analysis
- Monthly revenue trends
- Top 10 product categories by revenue
- Top 10 states by revenue
- Customer repeat-purchase analysis
- Payment method distribution
- On-time versus late deliveries

## 4. Key Findings
- Total product revenue was approximately R$13.59 million.
- Health & Beauty and Watches & Gifts were among the leading product categories.
- São Paulo generated the highest state-level revenue.
- Credit cards were the most frequently used payment method.
- Repeat customers represented a small share of customers.
- Most delivered orders arrived on time.

## 5. Conclusion
The project demonstrates how Python, SQL, and Power BI can be used together to analyze e-commerce data and generate business insights.

## 6. Dataset
Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset – Kaggle.
